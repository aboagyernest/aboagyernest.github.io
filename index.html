Ernest Aboagye, ASA

Senior Lecturer in Actuarial Science
Department of Mathematics and Statistics, Purdue University

eaboagye@purdue.edu · CV (PDF) · GitHub

I study who bears the largest insured losses and how risk transfer should be designed when the tail of the loss distribution is changing. My work combines insurance economics, actuarial science and the statistics of extremes, with applications to flood and crop insurance, cyber risk, and the systemic risk of insurers and banks. I received my Ph.D. in Risk Management and Insurance from Georgia State University in 2025, and before that I worked as an actuary at Willis Towers Watson, Milliman, New York Life and American Family Insurance.

Research Interests

  • Insurance and catastrophe risk: climate and flood risk, public insurance programs, cyber and data-breach risk, exposure-adjusted loss models
  • Risk transfer: optimal reinsurance and excess-of-loss contracts, risk measures, estimation risk in contract design
  • Financial econometrics and systemic risk: contagion and tail dependence, marginal expected shortfall, risk forecasting and backtesting
  • Statistics of extremes: peaks over threshold, multivariate regular variation, bootstrap inference for tail functionals
